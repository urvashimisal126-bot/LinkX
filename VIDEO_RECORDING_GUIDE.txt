LINKX — FRIENDLY VIDEO RECORDING GUIDE
=======================================

Goal: record a confident 2-minute demo. You only need a browser, the app, and
this guide. Do not explain code. Explain the officer’s next action.

BEFORE YOU RECORD
-----------------
1. Open a terminal in the LinkX folder and run: npm run dev
2. Open the local URL Vite prints (normally http://localhost:5173/).
3. Keep the browser window at 100% zoom and use a desktop-width window.
4. Start at the landing page. If you were already in the workspace, type the
   local URL without #/ and refresh.
5. Keep this guide nearby. You will click “Load sample case” only once.
6. Make sure browser notifications and unrelated tabs are hidden.

SHORT SCRIPT AND WHAT TO SHOW
-----------------------------

0:00–0:12 | LANDING PAGE
Show: the headline, the three statistics, and the Enter workspace button.
Say: “LinkX is an offline investigation workspace for cyber-fraud cases. It
turns a complaint into a clear freeze priority during the Golden Hour.”
Click: Enter workspace.

0:12–0:20 | OVERVIEW + LOAD DATA
Show: the empty overview first, then the Load sample case button.
Say: “An officer can load a complete case locally. For this demo, one click
loads all six evidence sources and their connected case data.”
Click: Load sample case.
Then say: “We immediately see six verified exhibits, nine resolved entities,
and the highest recovery priority.”

0:20–0:35 | EVIDENCE
Show: click Evidence in the left sidebar; let all six cards be visible.
Say: “Every source is registered and hash-verified: telecom, bank, IPDR,
mule records, exchange logs, and the malware manifest. The intake stays local.”
Point to: the Verified badges and abbreviated SHA-256 hash line.

0:35–0:50 | LINKS
Show: click Links. Point to the attacker-controlled number row.
Say: “LinkX explains every identity link. This attacker-controlled number is
resolved by an IMEI match across evidence E01 and E06 — not a guess or a black
box confidence score.”

0:50–1:25 | NETWORK — THE MAIN MOMENT
Show: click Network. Pause for the graph.
Say: “This is the money trail, from the victim’s account through layered mule
accounts to the final off-ramp.”
Click: the Victim account node.
Say: “Selecting any node exposes its identifiers and supporting exhibits.”
Click: Highlight full path.
Say: “With one action, the officer isolates the recovery path: victim, HDFC,
PNB, the fraud VPA, and finally the exchange wallet.”
Optional: click the ₹2,60,000 arrow between HDFC and PNB.
Say: “Each transfer keeps its amount, exact timestamp, and source exhibit.”

1:25–1:45 | RISK & FREEZE
Show: click Risk & freeze. Keep rank 1 in view.
Say: “The first recipient is not automatically the first account to freeze.
LinkX ranks where funds are still recoverable. PNB account ending 7790 is first:
score 96, residual funds, and high inbound velocity.”
Click: Recommend freeze.
Say: “The recommendation is recorded locally for the case officer. No live bank
instruction is sent from this demo.”

1:45–1:55 | TIMELINE
Show: click Timeline and scroll lightly from malware installation to complaint.
Say: “The timeline correlates calls, malware activity, bank movement, IPDR,
and the complaint down to the second. It makes the sequence easy to explain.”

1:55–2:05 | REPORT
Show: click Report; center the A4 preview.
Say: “Finally, LinkX turns the investigation into a printable brief: case
summary, entity evidence, fund flow, recovery recommendations, and exhibit
hashes.”
Click: Export brief. In the browser print window, choose Save as PDF if wanted.
End with: “From complaint to freeze order, inside the Golden Hour.”

BACKUP / TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------
- If the case is not populated, click Reload sample case on Overview or Evidence.
- If the graph is too zoomed in, use the target button at the bottom-left of the
  graph to fit it back on screen.
- Drag empty graph space to pan; use mouse wheel to zoom.
- If the sidebar labels are hidden, click the small panel icon near the LinkX
  logo to expand it.
- Refreshing the page resets the local demo. Start again at Overview and click
  Load sample case once.

ONE-SENTENCE CLOSE
------------------
“LinkX gives cyber-cell officers an offline, evidence-backed answer to the
most urgent question in the first hour: where should we act right now?”
